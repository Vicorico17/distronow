# Comment-to-email lead magnets

The goal is to move useful attention into an owned audience.

## Flow

```text
post → keyword comment → DM → email gate → instant delivery → nurture → offer
```

1. Create one narrow guide that solves the problem opened by the post.
2. Use one uppercase keyword across the video, caption, and automation.
3. Send the DM to a focused email page.
4. Deliver the guide immediately on the thank-you page and in the first email.
5. Nurture with value two or three times per week for one or two weeks.
6. Invite the person to the product, community, call, or next relevant step.

The keyword must have a real payoff. Measure comments, DM delivery, opt-ins,
delivery rate, nurture engagement, and conversion—not just views.
