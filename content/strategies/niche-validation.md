# Niche and content opportunity validation

Start with a seed, then define:

- exact audience and situation;
- recurring pain and emotional urgency;
- desired transformation;
- buyer proof and existing spending;
- possible offers;
- content angles and visual proof;
- risks and claim boundaries.

Score each idea for audience urgency, buyer evidence, specificity, content
depth, and monetization. Validate with customer comments, communities, reviews,
paid competitors, search questions, and existing offers.

The best opportunity has both content demand and a credible next step. Do not
choose a niche only because it is interesting or appears uncrowded.
