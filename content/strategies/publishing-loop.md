# Publishing and learning loop

```text
idea → draft → platform adaptation → review → approved → scheduled → published
→ metrics → insight → next idea
```

accman should manage accounts, platform-specific previews, media validation,
calendar slots, recurring posts, content sets, failures, retries, and account
health. Every result should feed back into DistroNow.

Track winning and losing hooks, angles, formats, channels, audiences, CTAs,
publishing times, and conversion paths. Record enough evidence to explain why a
recommendation was made. Keep human approval before publishing, outreach,
replies, livestream chat, or other external actions.
