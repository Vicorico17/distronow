# Virality and shareability

Virality is a testing discipline, not permission to mislead people.

## 30-day question-hook series

1. Ask one sharp question tied to a real customer problem.
2. Establish the before-state quickly.
3. Introduce tension or the common mistaken belief.
4. Give one useful insight or mechanism.
5. End with a payoff, proof point, or natural next step.

Keep the first scene specific. Use 7–13 words for a mid-scene idea when a
short-form post needs a readable overlay. Test one variable at a time: opening
frame, hook type, emotional driver, proof, format, or CTA.

## Share triggers

- recognition: “that is exactly what happens to me”;
- usefulness: a framework someone wants to save;
- identity: a belief or group the audience wants to represent;
- surprise: a non-obvious mechanism or contrast;
- proof: a result people can explain to someone else.

Avoid fake urgency, invented proof, unsupported claims, empty ragebait, and
comment bait without a real payoff.
