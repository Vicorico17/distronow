# Slideshows and carousels

Every slide has one job:

1. hook attention with pain, result, emotion, proof, or curiosity;
2. move the viewer forward with short readable text;
3. make the image support the point;
4. end with a natural next step.

## QA rules

- Hook: approximately 5–12 words.
- Body slide: approximately 4–10 words.
- CTA: approximately 5–15 words.
- One main text item per slide.
- Use sentence case unless the brand has a deliberate alternative.
- Keep text in image negative space and away from faces, hands, and product proof.
- Images should show pain, proof, product, process, before/after, audience
  context, or a real comment/result—not act as generic decoration.
- Score hook strength, text clarity, visual relevance, placement, voice, and CTA
  from 1–5 before approval.
