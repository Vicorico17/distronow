# Characters and UGC production

## Character bible

Define the role, audience read, primary desire, hidden need, fear, pressure,
stakes, contradiction, vocabulary, rhythm, humor, visual silhouette, palette,
wardrobe, signature prop, default expression, and body language.

Keep these anchors stable across variations:

- core desire;
- silhouette and palette;
- speech rhythm;
- signature behavior or prop;
- do-not-change list.

## UGC workflow

1. Research the audience and competitors.
2. Select a winning angle.
3. Write three hook variations.
4. Create the creator/character brief.
5. Plan eight scenes with voiceover, b-roll, overlays, and CTA.
6. Capture or generate assets in 9:16.
7. Edit, review claims, and create platform variants.
8. Publish a controlled test and record results by hook, angle, and format.

UGC should feel specific and human. Do not use fake testimonials, celebrity
likenesses, invented results, or medical/financial claims without support.
