# Content mix

Use this as a starting allocation, then change it based on performance and the
business objective.

## 50% Growth

Job: reach new people and make the right audience stop.

- pain-specific hooks;
- result-first posts;
- contrarian positions;
- POVs, listicles, stories, and pattern interruptions;
- one clear audience problem per post.

## 35% Trust

Job: make the brand credible and useful.

- frameworks and how-tos;
- product or screen demonstrations;
- teardowns and comparisons;
- founder perspective;
- proof, customer language, and honest limitations.

## 15% Conversion

Job: give a warmed audience a natural next step.

- testimonials and case studies;
- before/after stories;
- soft offer explanations;
- lead magnets;
- clear, specific CTAs.

Every campaign should state its mix, audience stage, proof requirement, and
success metric before generation begins.
